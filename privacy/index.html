Privacy Policy — Fastwell - Longevity

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Fastwell - Longevity (“Fastwell”, “the app”) is made by Mia Ipsen, an independent developer based in Denmark. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and what choices you have.

Short version: Your fasts stay on your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and the app does not connect to Apple Health. The app sends anonymous usage statistics (for example, “a fast was started”) so it can be improved. These never include your fasting times, durations or plans.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is:

Mia Ipsen
Denmark
Email: ipsenmia90@gmail.com

2. Your fasts stay on your device

Everything you log in Fastwell is stored only on your device. This includes your fasting plan, the start and end times of your fasts, your history, streaks and milestones, and your settings. We never receive, read, store or transmit it. There is no account and no server holding your fasts.

Fastwell does not read from or write to Apple Health (HealthKit) and does not collect any health, body or biometric data.

Your data may be included in your device’s normal backups, such as iCloud Backup or a computer backup, depending on your device settings. Those backups are handled by Apple or by you under your own settings, not by us.

3. Data we do collect: usage analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used, so we can fix confusing parts and decide what to improve. We use the version of the Firebase SDK without ad measurement. It does not access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), and the app does not track you across other apps or websites.

What is sent

  • Usage events that record that an action happened in the app: onboarding started, viewed and completed; a fast was started, completed, added, edited or deleted; a fast’s start time was edited; notifications were allowed or declined; and a rating prompt was requested. Firebase also records standard events such as first open, app session start and app update.
  • Technical information: an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase, device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate country or region (derived from your IP address, which is not stored).

What is never sent

  • the start or end times, durations or goals of your fasts
  • your fasting plan, history, streaks or milestones
  • your name, email address, contacts, location or any other information that identifies you

Analytics data is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or tracking.

Your choice: analytics is controlled by Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data. When it is off, Fastwell sends no analytics events at all, including the standard ones Firebase records automatically.

  • EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland: analytics is off until you agree. The app asks once, and you can say no without losing any features. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Everywhere else: analytics is on by default, and you can turn it off at any time. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used so we can maintain and improve it (Art. 6(1)(f)).

The app decides which group you are in from the region set on your device (Settings → General → Language & Region), not from your location. You can withdraw consent or object at any time; see section 7.

Retention: analytics data is kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically.

4. Reminders

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example, when you reach your fasting goal) are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content is sent to us or to any server.

5. Ratings, feedback and crash reports

  • Ratings: the app may show Apple’s standard rating prompt. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple; we only see what Apple shows to developers in App Store Connect.
  • Feedback by email: if you email us, for example from Settings → Support → Send feedback, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the app, and delete it when it is no longer needed. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Crash reports: Fastwell has no crash-reporting SDK of its own. If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in your iPhone’s privacy settings, Apple may share anonymous crash and diagnostic reports with us under Apple’s privacy policy.

6. Who processes data on our behalf

Analytics is provided by Google (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EU/EEA and Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere). Google acts as our data processor. Data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. These transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and similar laws elsewhere, such as Mexico’s data protection law and US state privacy laws) you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • have it corrected or deleted
  • restrict or object to its processing
  • data portability

Because analytics data is not linked to your identity, we usually cannot identify which data belongs to you. If you contact us, we will help as far as we reasonably can (GDPR Art. 11).

You can always:

  • delete all your fasts in the app, one by one in History, or all at once by deleting the app (and any device backups that contain it)
  • withdraw your consent to analytics, or object to it, by turning off Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data in the app. From then on no analytics events are sent, and the analytics data stored on your device, including its identifier, is reset. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.
  • reset your analytics identifier by deleting and reinstalling the app, which creates a new, unrelated identifier

To exercise your other rights, or if you prefer to object by email, contact ipsenmia90@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in Denmark or elsewhere in the EU, you can also complain to a data protection authority. In Denmark this is Datatilsynet (www.datatilsynet.dk).

8. Children

Fastwell is not directed at children and is rated 16+ on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Not medical advice

Fastwell is a personal timer, not medical advice. Talk with a qualified healthcare professional before changing how you eat or fast, especially if you are pregnant, managing a health condition or taking medication.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
