I forgot to press start. Can I fix the start time?

Yes. While a fast is running, tap the start time on the Home screen and set an earlier time. You can also add or edit past fasts in History.

I’m not getting a reminder when I reach my goal

Reminders need notification permission. Open the iPhone Settings app → Notifications → Fastwell and turn on Allow Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device, so they also respect Focus modes and Do Not Disturb.

How do I change my fasting plan?

Choose a different plan (16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD or custom) in the app. A new plan applies from your next fast.

How do I restore a purchase?

There are no in-app purchases in this version of Fastwell, so there is nothing to restore.

How do I delete my data?

Your fasts are stored only on your device. We never receive them. You can delete individual fasts in History, or delete all of them by deleting the app. Your fasts may also be in your device backups, such as iCloud Backup or a computer backup. Delete those backups too if you want the data gone from them.

For details about the anonymous usage analytics the app sends, see the Privacy Policy.

Will my fasts move to a new iPhone?

There is no account or cloud sync. Your fasts move to a new iPhone only if you restore it from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one.

Important

Fastwell is a personal timer, not medical advice. Talk with a qualified healthcare professional before changing how you eat or fast, especially if you are pregnant, managing a health condition or taking medication. Listen to your body and don’t push for longer than feels right for you.
